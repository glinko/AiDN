# M1.8 Prototype A performance and UX gate

**Status:** Verified implementation slice  
**Scope:** Baseline gate for the synthetic Spatial Core; not a production SLO.  
**Depends on:** M1.1–M1.7 and [ADR-014](./ADR-014-spatial-ui-technical-architecture.md)

Prototype A ends with a visible, bounded gate rather than an unverified claim.
`performance.ts` samples at most 24 frames while the document is visible,
pauses on `visibilitychange`/`document.hidden`, records best-effort heap size,
and updates pointer/focus latency when a local interaction completes. The DOM
readout shows the target budget and whether the current bounded sample needs
attention.

The gate targets are approximately 60 FPS, 16.7 ms average frame time, and
under 100 ms pointer/focus response. The initial fixture is a deterministic
60 FPS/16.2 ms baseline so the route remains inspectable in browsers without a
performance-memory API. It is not a substitute for a device lab run; a future
profiling pass can replace the sample values without changing the contract.

## UX/accessibility checks

- Desktop and mobile profiles are exercised by the Prototype A Playwright
  suite, including the mobile DPR/fog budget.
- Reduced motion makes Primary Agent channels static and camera transitions
  immediate; the existing Spatial accessibility suite covers the route.
- The canvas stays out of the accessibility tree, while DOM labels, buttons,
  entity list, and gate remain native and keyboard reachable.
- Forced colors/high contrast and reduced transparency remain governed by the
  Spatial theme boundary; no new global styles are introduced.
- Bundle budget and Classic/Spatial lazy loading remain guarded by the M0.5
  report and build checks.
